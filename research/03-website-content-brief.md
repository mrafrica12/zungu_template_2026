## Editorial guardrails

Do not invent prices, room labels, luxury/star classifications, free services, guarantees or amenities. Keep historical photographs separate from confirmed current offerings. A WhatsApp enquiry is not an instant confirmed reservation. No online payment or booking engine has been verified.
