## Provenance and publication status

[The JSON manifest](media-manifest.json) records source page, exact downloaded image URL, dimensions, byte size, SHA-256 checksum, uploader, displayed date, quality notes and rights status for each Maps file. Original-size variants were requested from the observed Google-hosted image URLs. No Google branding, map tiles or third-party stock imagery were collected as hotel assets.

Maps attribution to the business profile does not establish copyright ownership. Confirm hotel reuse rights and that scenes remain current before publication. Photo 03 is stored separately as a third-party research reference.

The accessible gallery stopped at ten photographs; requesting further photos opened a sign-in prompt. The remaining 50 photographs were not collected or assessed. Instagram grid previews were compressed and not added to the high-quality gallery.
