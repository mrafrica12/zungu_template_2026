Privacy Policy

Updated 25 October 2026

Your enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message using the details you enter, such as your name, stay dates, guest count and optional notes. The website does not submit these fields to a website database. You review and send the message in WhatsApp. Opening WhatsApp passes the draft to that service. Please avoid entering sensitive information.

A draft or message is an enquiry, not a confirmed reservation or payment. The hotel confirms availability and rates directly.

Saved places

The neighbourhood guide stores your saved place identifiers in your browser on this device. You can remove saved places in the guide or clear your browser’s site data. Sharing the list prepares a WhatsApp draft.

Third-party services

Google Fonts and embedded Google Maps receive connection information, such as your IP address, when loaded. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps and other websites are subject to those services’ privacy policies. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs to deliver and secure the site.

Analytics

Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager are not currently activated on this website. This notice will be updated before analytics is enabled.

Contact and requests

For questions about personal information shared with the hotel, or to request correction or deletion of an enquiry, contact Zungu Hotel, Goba, near Ndambi Bus Stop, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, on +255 766 003 050. Ask the hotel about its retention of messages and reservation records.
